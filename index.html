About me

I am a master’s student in economics at Linköping University, specialising in financial economics. From January 2027 I will be an intern at the Parliamentary Research Service of the Swedish Parliament. I completed my undergraduate studies in economics at Linköping University and in international social science, with a focus on political science, at Linnaeus University, including an exchange semester at Southwestern University in Texas.

My interests lie where macroeconomics meets international politics, in particular monetary policy, exchange rate regimes and trade. I am drawn to questions about how economic policy is shaped and what its effects look like in the data, and I work mainly with panel and time series methods in R and Python.

Writing and projects

  1. Optimum Currency Areas in Practice: A Panel Data Analysis of Labour Mobility in the EMU, with Hannes Bokne.
    Bachelor’s thesis in economics, Linköping University, 2026. [DiVA]
  2. Makroterminalen, an analysis platform for macroeconomic data.
    Ongoing since 2026. A Python platform that brings together economic, financial and political series from open sources such as Statistics Sweden, Eurostat, the OECD, the ECB, the IMF and the World Bank in one common register, so that a claim can be tested against data in minutes.

Engagement

Since 2024 I have been coordinator for Economic Security Research at Youth Security Europe, a European network active in more than ten countries. In 2023–24 I was a board member of the Foreign Policy Association at Linnaeus University, where I was responsible for its podcast and interviewed members of the Swedish Parliament, members of the European Parliament and other experts on economic development and geopolitical conflict. In 2024 I took part in ConSIMium, a simulation of negotiations in the Council of the European Union organised with the Council’s General Secretariat in Brussels.

I speak Swedish and English, and also Spanish and Danish.
