About me

I am a student in the Master’s Programme in Economics at Linköping University, specialising in financial economics. From January 2027 I will be an intern at the Parliamentary Research Service of the Swedish Parliament. I completed my undergraduate studies in economics at Linköping University and in international social science, with a focus on political science, at Linnaeus University, including an exchange semester at Southwestern University in Texas.

Research interests include international macroeconomics, geoeconomics and trade policy, with particular focus on exchange rate regimes, currency unions, tariffs and the use of economic interdependence as leverage between states. Empirical work is based mainly on panel and time series methods in R and Python.

Engagement

From 2024 to 2026 I was coordinator for Economic Security Research at Youth Security Europe, a European network active in more than ten countries. In 2023–24 I was a board member of the Foreign Policy Association at Linnaeus University, where I was responsible for its podcast and interviewed members of the Swedish Parliament, members of the European Parliament and other experts on economic development and geopolitical conflict. In 2024 I took part in ConSIMium, a simulation of negotiations in the Council of the European Union organised with the Council’s General Secretariat in Brussels.

I speak Swedish and English, and also Spanish and Danish.
